HOW PHONE EMAIL & OTP DELIVERY WORKS WITH RESEND (arabsignature.com):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Resend is an OUTBOUND transactional email sender. Connecting "arabsignature.com"
   to Resend means your website now sends official emails FROM:
   - concierge@arabsignature.com (for guest room bookings & food/drink orders)
   - security@arabsignature.com  (for 6-digit Accountant & CEO OTP passcodes)
   to ANY email address in the world (Gmail, Yahoo, iCloud, Outlook, etc.).

2. How the Accountant receives the 6-Digit OTP on his phone:
   - Resend sends emails; it is not a phone mail inbox app.
   - Therefore, on the Accountant Login page (or inside the Admin Portal -> Staff
     Credentials tab), there is a "Phone Email Inbox (Receives 6-Digit OTP)" field.
   - The Accountant logs in with his official ID (accountant@arabsignature.com)
     and enters the personal Gmail/Yahoo address that is already logged into his
     phone. "security@arabsignature.com" immediately delivers the 6-digit OTP
     straight to his phone's mail app!
   - (Optional): If you also want emails sent to "accountant@arabsignature.com"
     from outside to land on his phone, simply turn on free Email Forwarding in
     your domain DNS (Cloudflare / Namecheap / cPanel) to forward
     accountant@arabsignature.com -> his personal Gmail.
